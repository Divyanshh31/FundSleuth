Understanding Mutual Funds & Financial Traps

Everything you need to know about Net Asset Value, portfolio duplication, and hidden distributor fees.

1. What is a Mutual Fund?

A mutual fund pools money from thousands of investors to purchase a diversified basket of stocks, bonds, or commodities managed by professional fund managers.

2. Direct vs Regular Plans

Regular Plans pay 1% to 1.5% annual distributor commissions to brokers. Direct Plans have zero commission, saving you tens of thousands in compounded wealth!

3. The Overlap Trap

Buying 5 different large-cap funds often gives false diversification. Most fund managers buy the exact same top 10 stocks (HDFC Bank, Reliance, ICICI Bank), doubling your risk!

4. Toxic Stock Holdings

Fund managers sometimes hold underperforming or loss-making companies. FundSleuth audits underlying stock P/L metrics to alert you immediately.
