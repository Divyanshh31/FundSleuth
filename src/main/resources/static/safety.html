5 Golden Rules of Safe Mutual Fund Investing

1
No Fixed Guarantees

SEBI regulations strictly prohibit mutual funds or advisors from guaranteeing fixed monthly or yearly returns. Any scheme offering guaranteed 20-30% return is illegal fraud.

2
Always Choose Direct

Regular funds deduct up to 1.5% annual distributor commissions from your fund's NAV. Direct plans have zero commission, yielding significantly higher long-term compounding.

3
Check Stock Overlap

Buying 10 different Large Cap funds doesn't reduce risk if all 10 hold Reliance, HDFC, and ICICI Bank. Use FundSleuth's Portfolio X-Ray to spot hidden concentration.

4
Direct AMC Payments

Mutual fund SIP payments should ONLY be made directly to official AMC bank accounts or SEBI-registered RTAs (CAMS/KFintech). Never send funds to individual agent UPI handles.

5
Verify SEBI Registration

Before accepting advisory services, check if the entity holds a valid SEBI RIA (Registered Investment Adviser) or Research Analyst registration on the official SEBI portal.
